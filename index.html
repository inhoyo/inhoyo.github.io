Research

Areas of Focus

01

Terahertz Wireless Communications

Experimental high-speed transmission systems in the 300 GHz (WR-3) band. Photonic upconversion via PIN photodiode transmitters with demonstrated throughputs exceeding 109 Gbit/s, and a record 221 Gb/s dual-polarisation link over 500 m.

02

Probabilistic Constellation Shaping & Rate Adaptation

PCS enables flexible, rate-adaptive modulation by biasing symbol distributions toward the Shannon limit. I study time-adaptive PCS for combined optical/THz links and neural-network-based mitigation of photodiode nonlinearities.

03

Converged Optical & THz Links for 6G

Seamless integration of THz wireless segments into fiber-optic backhaul targeting 6G requirements. Adaptive baseband techniques for converged links — the core topic of my PhD dissertation at TU Berlin.
