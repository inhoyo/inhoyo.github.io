Profile

Professional Profile

I am a Scientist at the Fraunhofer Institute for Telecommunications, Heinrich Hertz Institute (HHI), Berlin, within the Department of Photonic Networks and Systems, where I have been working since July 2021. In parallel, I am pursuing a PhD (Dr.-Ing.) at Technische Universität Berlin (TUB) under the working title “Investigating Adaptive Baseband Techniques for Converged Optical / THz Wireless Links.”

My research focuses on high-capacity Terahertz (THz) wireless communications and their integration into next-generation 6G photonic network infrastructures. I develop advanced DSP algorithms for rate-adaptive THz links and conduct experimental characterisation of photonic transmitter components for probabilistically shaped, high-speed THz wireless transmission beyond 100 Gbit/s.

Alongside my research role, I serve as a University Lecturer at HTW Berlin and BHT Berlin, covering communications engineering and signal processing at Bachelor’s and Master’s level.
